Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

Discover Performance Advisory ("DPA", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and manage your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using our website at discoverperformance.com.au or enquiring about or registering for any of our programs, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Discover Performance Advisory is a training and advisory business based in Sydney, NSW, Australia. We offer corporate training programs, short term rental (STR) programs, and advisory services to individuals and organisations.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Identity information: your full name
  • Contact information: email address and phone number
  • Payment information: when you purchase a program, payment is processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your full credit card details. Stripe handles and stores payment data in accordance with their own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance standards
  • Communication records: emails, enquiry form submissions, or other correspondence you send us
  • Website usage data: if we enable analytics (such as Google Analytics), we may collect non-identifying information about how you use our site, including pages visited, time spent, and device type

3. How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information in the following ways:

  • When you submit an enquiry or contact form on our website
  • When you register or pay for one of our programs (via Stripe)
  • When you email or call us directly
  • Through website analytics tools (if enabled)

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary for our functions and activities. We do not collect sensitive information (such as health or financial records) unless you provide it voluntarily and it is directly relevant to the services we are providing.

4. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you have requested
  • Process program registrations and payments
  • Send you program-related communications, such as confirmation emails and course materials
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations
  • If you have opted in, send you marketing communications about upcoming programs and services (you may opt out at any time)

We will not use your personal information for any purpose that is unrelated to the above without first seeking your consent.

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information to:

  • Stripe Inc.: our payment processor, for the purpose of processing program registrations and payments securely. Stripe's Privacy Policy is available at stripe.com/au/privacy
  • Google LLC: if Google Analytics is enabled on our website, Google may receive de-identified usage data. Google's Privacy Policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Professional advisors: such as lawyers or accountants, where necessary and subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Government or regulatory bodies: where required or authorised by law

Where we disclose personal information to third parties, we take reasonable steps to ensure those parties handle the information in a manner consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

6. Overseas Disclosure

Some of our third-party service providers (including Stripe and Google) are based or operate overseas, including in the United States. By using our website and services, you consent to your personal information being processed by these providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to ensure these providers protect your information in a manner that is consistent with Australian privacy law.

7. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Your information may be stored electronically (including via email, cloud services, or third-party platforms such as Stripe).

Whilst we use reasonable measures to protect your information, no data transmission or storage system is completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as it is needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

9. Your Rights: Access and Correction

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct any inaccurate, out-of-date or incomplete information
  • Request that we delete your personal information, where it is no longer required
  • Withdraw consent to receive marketing communications at any time

To make a request, please contact us at jstibbard@discoverperformance.com. We will respond within 30 days. In some circumstances we may decline a request. If we do, we will explain why.

10. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyse site usage. If we implement Google Analytics, it will collect anonymised data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time on site. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some parts of our website may not function correctly if you do.

11. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page with the date it was last updated. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or mishandled your personal information, please contact us at jstibbard@discoverperformance.com. We will investigate and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.
