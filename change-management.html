About the Program

Lead change.
Bring people with you.

Change is constant, but knowing how to manage it effectively is a skill. This program gives participants a structured framework for navigating organisational change, from planning and communication through to embedding and sustaining new behaviours.

Using a real-world Case Study approach, participants practise applying the tools in a realistic context, building both confidence and capability they can use from day one.

"Most change initiatives fail not because of the plan, but because of how people are brought through it. This program changes that."

— Jeff Stibbard, Program Facilitator

Program At a Glance
Who It's For
Leaders, managers and project sponsors responsible for guiding teams through change
Format
In-person, tailored to your organisation's context and change agenda
Includes
Change Toolkit and Case Study materials
Program Outcomes

Upon completion, participants will be able to:

01

Identify the steps of a Change Management Framework

Understand the end-to-end process of managing change, from diagnosis and planning through to communication, implementation and review.

02

Develop skills through a real-world Case Study

Apply the framework in a realistic, hands-on scenario, building practical capability and confidence that transfers directly to your own organisation's challenges.

03

Describe how Change Management and Project Management fit together

Understand the relationship and boundaries between the two disciplines, and why integrating them leads to better outcomes for both people and projects.

04

Use the Change Toolkit

Leave with a practical set of tools and templates you can apply immediately, including stakeholder mapping, impact assessments, communication and resistance plans.

05

Monitor and embed change for lasting impact

Know how to track progress, identify slippage early and sustain new behaviours over time, so change sticks well after the project closes.

Why It Matters

Change doesn't fail on paper.
It fails with people.

This program gives leaders the language, tools and frameworks to close that gap and bring their teams through change with confidence.
