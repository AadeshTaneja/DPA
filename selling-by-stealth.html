About the Program

Get under the radar.
Win more business.

Selling by Stealth™ is not a conventional sales program. It's a mindset shift. It's built on the insight that the best salespeople don't feel like salespeople to the customer - they feel like trusted partners who genuinely understand their world.

Over 90 days, this program equips your team with the strategies, tools and psychology to embed themselves with customers, spot unmet needs, and convert those insights into sustainable revenue growth.

"The difference between getting the sale and losing it often isn't price or product. It's trust. This program teaches you how to build it before you even ask for the order."

- Jeff Stibbard, Author & Program Facilitator

Core Principles

The Stealth Approach

Get Under the Radar

The best salespeople don't announce themselves. They show up as consultants, advisors and problem-solvers and earn the sale before they ever ask for it.

Identify Unmet Needs

Customers rarely know what they don't know. Learn to ask the right questions, listen deeply and surface the opportunities hiding in plain sight within every relationship.

Embed with Your Customers

Great account management isn't maintenance. It's expansion. Build the strategies to make your organisation feel like an extension of your customer's own team.

Sell High Enough

Are you having the right conversations with the right people? Learn to navigate decision-maker landscapes and ensure you're engaging at the level that matters.

Convert Insights into Revenue

Information without action is just data. This program closes the loop, giving you the tools to take every insight and convert it into a meaningful revenue opportunity.

Build Lasting Confidence

Selling feels different when it comes from authenticity and genuine understanding. This program builds the kind of confidence that grows with every customer interaction.

The Competitive Edge
